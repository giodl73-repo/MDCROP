## Integration

- ROLES: `.roles/ROLE.md` covers context boundaries, corpus health, retrieval
  benchmarking, provenance linkage, view contracts, performance budgets, and
  README/CLI/fixture editorial review.
- VTRACE: MDCROP does not currently carry a repo-local VTRACE matrix; PITFALL
  entries cite specs, wave evidence, roles, and executable validation until a
  dedicated trace slice exists.
- Tests: Rust tests, clippy, view/status CLI smokes, benchmark fixture runs,
  strict view-store inspection, and PITFALL validators are the evidence hooks.
